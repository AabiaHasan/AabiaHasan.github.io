---
This is the first entry in what I'm hoping becomes a running record of the
work — not just the finished photos, but the process behind them: the
locations I go back to, the light I keep chasing, and the shots that didn't
make the gallery but taught me something anyway.

Expect a mix of things here:

- Notes from specific shoots and the decisions behind them
- Favorite locations, revisited across seasons
- The occasional gear or workflow post

Thanks for reading — and for looking at the photos in the first place.

*New entries show up here automatically. To publish one, drop a new
Markdown file into `src/content/blog/`, named like
`YYYY-MM-DD-your-title.md`, with a `title`, `date`, and `excerpt` at the
top between `---` lines.*
